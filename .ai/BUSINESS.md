# BUSINESS

## Project

Moratta Imóveis

---

# Mission

Help people achieve home ownership through a simple, trustworthy and technology-driven buying experience.

The website exists to generate qualified leads, educate buyers and convert visitors into conversations.

---

# Business Model

Moratta is a real estate agency.

It does NOT build properties.

It markets and sells properties from partner developers.

---

# Primary Goal

Generate qualified leads.

Every page should increase the probability that a visitor contacts the agency.

---

# Success Metrics

The website is successful when it increases:

- WhatsApp conversations
- Lead form submissions
- Credit analysis requests
- Property visits
- Sales conversions

Not when it simply receives page views.

---

# Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Brazilian families
- First-time home buyers
- Minha Casa Minha Vida customers
- CLT workers
- Buyers with low or medium income
- People who need financing

Secondary audience:

- Investors
- Buyers of higher-value properties

---

# Geographic Area

Primary region:

- Gravataí
- Cachoeirinha
- Canoas
- Alvorada
- Porto Alegre
- Viamão
- Guaíba
- Sapucaia do Sul
- Esteio
- São Leopoldo

Future expansion is allowed.

---

# Communication Style

The website must communicate:

- Trust
- Simplicity
- Professionalism
- Transparency
- Speed

Avoid:

- Aggressive marketing
- False urgency
- Clickbait
- Excessive animations

---

# Brand Position

Moratta is positioned as a modern real estate company.

Not a luxury brand.

Not a discount website.

The experience should feel premium while remaining accessible.

---

# Competitive Advantages

The company differentiates itself through:

- Personalized service
- Financing assistance
- Local expertise
- Fast response
- Professional guidance

---

# Website Priorities

Priority order:

1. Generate leads
2. Explain the buying process
3. Present developments
4. Build trust
5. Strengthen the brand

---

# Future Vision

The website will eventually become part of the Atlas ecosystem.

Future integrations may include:

- CRM
- Customer portal
- Property management
- AI assistant
- WhatsApp automation
- Analytics
- CMS

Current implementation should already be prepared for this evolution.

---

# What Should Never Happen

The AI must never transform the website into:

- an online marketplace
- a generic property listing portal
- a blog-focused website
- a news website

The website is a lead generation platform.

---

# Decision Rule

Whenever there is uncertainty between two implementation options:

Choose the option that:

- improves maintainability
- improves scalability
- improves conversion
- improves user experience
- preserves the existing architecture
